### MSRV

The current MSRV of this crate is 1.96. The MSRV is guaranteed to not exceed
`<current stable release> - 7`, though in practice it may be lower than this -
your mileage may vary. If we change this policy in a backwards incompatible way
(e.g. changing it to `<current stable release> - 1`), this constitutes a
breaking change, and would be a major version bump (e.g. 1.1 -> 2.0).
